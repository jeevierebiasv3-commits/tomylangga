( chapter one — the beginning )

“From that van ride to the resort, I knew there was something different about you. The way you lit up when we talked about CODM; and how easy our conversation felt; made the whole trip unforgettable” — Jeevie 💙

First meeting
May 14, 2022
San Vicente Ferrer Parish
Buanoy, Balamban, Cebu

It was a bright Saturday at my mother’s nephew’s wedding. I was just a guest, but from the moment I saw her standing with the bridesmaids, I kept stealing quick glances. She looked incredible in her dress, and every time she laughed it felt like the whole place lit up. We didn’t speak during the ceremony or the reception, though I kept noticing her from across the crowd.

Later, everyone decided to head to a nearby resort to keep the celebration going. She was the first to sit in the back of the van. When I climbed in, I slid into the seat beside her and pulled out my phone to play Call of Duty Mobile. After a few minutes she leaned a little closer and said with a smile, “You play that too? I play CODM as well.” That simple question started everything. We talked about the game; favorite maps, funny matches; and soon the conversation moved beyond gaming. The ride to the resort felt short, but it was enough to turn a quiet trip into the beginning of something I’ll never forget.

First Date
/01 — April 2023 · Toledo City

I’ll never forget our first date during the Fiesta in Toledo City. We met in front of the church while the whole place was alive with lights, music, and food stalls. We walked around, grabbed something to eat, and just talked like we’d known each other forever. We rode the ferris wheel and watched the city lights from the top. The breeze was cool, and everything felt easy and fun. Later, I finally held your hand—it just felt right. We walked all the way from Toledo back home still holding hands, and that simple walk meant more to me than anything else that night. It was the kind of moment you don’t forget.

First Concert Date
/02 — March 2020 · Toledo Sports Center

Our first concert date was at the Toledo Sports Center. The place was packed and the music was loud, but it felt like it was just the two of us. We found a good spot, shared snacks, and sang along to the songs we both knew. At one point you grabbed my hand when the crowd got excited, and you never let go. We laughed, shouted the lyrics, and just enjoyed every moment. That night wasn’t just about the music—it was about us, and I’ll always remember it.

Every Visit Matters
/03 — Every Weekend · Home

I’m really thankful every time you come over to my house. I know it’s not always easy, but you still make the effort just to be with me, and that means so much. Whenever you’re here, everything feels lighter; whether we’re laughing, eating, or just cuddling with ehem aw hahaha. It might look simple to others, but for me, it’s one of the best things. Your effort to be here never goes unnoticed, and I honestly feel so lucky to have you.

Best Birthday
/04 — November 2024

My birthday was one of the best days of my life because you were there to celebrate it with me. From the moment you showed up, everything already felt different—I was just so happy knowing I’d get to spend the day with you. What made it even more special was the fun we had at the pool. I really enjoyed swimming with you, racing around, and laughing together. The slides were such a blast too—I’ll never forget how we screamed and laughed like little kids. It wasn’t really the food, the gifts, or the party that made that day unforgettable. It was you being there, sharing every little moment with me. You made me feel loved, happy, and complete. That birthday will always be one of my favorite memories, and it’s all because you were a part of it.
